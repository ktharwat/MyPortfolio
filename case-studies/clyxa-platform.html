The Clyxa platform.

Three products on one platform for a digital marketing agency: the public site, a complete admin panel, and a real-time campaign tracker — all running in production today.

clyxa.org ↗ admin.clyxa.org ↗

Context

Clyxa is a digital marketing agency that sells performance for clients — but was operating its own business on scattered tools and spreadsheets, with nothing to show technically.

Challenge

The agency needed one coherent platform: a credible public presence, an operations brain for clients/projects/campaigns, and proof of campaign results its clients could see — not screenshots in emails.

Constraints

  • Small-team speed: ship in weeks, not enterprise quarters
  • Real-time visibility per campaign, from day one
  • Fully responsive experience — most agency traffic is mobile

My role

Architect and hands-on developer across all three products — deliberately outside my pharma domain, proving the same judgment transfers: model the domain, pick boring tools that fit, ship end to end.

Decisions

Node.js + Supabase instead of my default enterprise stack.

For a small product iterating weekly, managed Postgres and JS everywhere beat .NET's operational weight. Tool choice is a function of team size and change rate — not habit.

One admin brain, not three dashboards.

Clients, projects and campaigns share one relational core, so every screen answers cross-questions ("this client's active campaigns") without exports or glue scripts.

Campaign tracking built into the platform, not bolted on.

Per-campaign links and real-time reporting were day-one requirements. An agency whose platform proves results sells differently than one that promises them.

Execution

Main website first — modern, fully responsive, presenting services and packages interactively. Then the admin panel on Node.js/Supabase with REST APIs for the full client-project-campaign lifecycle. Then the tracker: campaign-scoped links feeding real-time analytics and performance reports.

Business impact

The agency runs its entire client lifecycle on its own platform, and shows live campaign evidence to prospects. The public site doubles as the technical credibility proof for the agency itself.

Lessons

Building for yourself is the fastest requirements workshop that exists. Every hour of ambiguity I would normally spend clarifying with a client disappeared — because the client was the mirror.
