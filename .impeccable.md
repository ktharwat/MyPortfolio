# Design Context for KeroProfile

## Design Context

### Users
Potential employers and freelance clients who need to evaluate Kyrellos Tharwat's 12+ years of software engineering expertise. Visitors seek to understand his technical capabilities, leadership experience, and determine if he's the right fit for their projects or teams.

### Brand Personality
**Professional, Expert, Leader** - A senior software engineer who demonstrates technical mastery, team leadership, and delivers reliable solutions. The brand conveys confidence, competence, and approachability for both employment opportunities and freelance projects.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern, sophisticated portfolio with dark hero section and light content areas. Purple-blue gradient color palette (#667eea to #764ba2) creates visual impact while maintaining professional credibility. Bilingual Arabic/English support with RTL/LTR layouts is essential. The design balances technical professionalism with contemporary web aesthetics.

### Design Principles
1. **Professional Credibility First**: Every design choice must reinforce expertise and trustworthiness for potential employers and clients
2. **Bilingual Excellence**: Seamless Arabic and English experiences with proper RTL/LTR support and cultural sensitivity
3. **Experience Showcase**: Visual hierarchy that immediately highlights 12+ years of experience and leadership achievements
4. **Contact-Driven**: Design must guide visitors toward email/phone contact for interviews and project discussions
5. **Performance & Accessibility**: Fast-loading, accessible static site that works across all devices and connection speeds

## Technical Guidelines
- Maintain static HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture
- Use Cairo and Tajawal fonts for Arabic content
- Preserve gradient accent colors and dark/light section contrast
- Ensure smooth animations enhance rather than distract from content
- Optimize for both desktop and mobile viewing experiences
- Keep file sizes minimal for fast loading globally
